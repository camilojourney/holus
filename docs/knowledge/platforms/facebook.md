## What Facebook Is Good For

Long-form content that earns a share. The audience here is slightly older and more likely to be decision-makers who forward content to colleagues. The goal is not virality — it is shareability.

## Post Structure

1. **Open** with a clear statement of what this post is about. No mystery, no withholding.
2. **Body** 3–5 short paragraphs. One idea per paragraph. Specific and concrete.
3. **Close** with exactly one "why this matters" sentence — the so-what for someone who might share it.
4. **Hashtags** at the very end: 3–5 max. Never more.

## Tone

- More structured than Threads, less formal than LinkedIn
- EN (Camilo Experience): confident, shareable, decision-maker register
- ES (Camilo Journey): same structure but warmer, more natural Spanish, community feel

## Hard Rules

- **No exclamation marks.** Confidence does not shout.
- **3–5 hashtags max.** No hashtag stacks.
- **Never exceed 5 hashtags** — it reads as spam.
- The "why this matters" close should be a statement, not a question.

## Hashtags

- EN: professional/industry tags (#AIImplementation #TechLeadership #AIEngineering)
- ES: Spanish-first where possible (#TecnologíaIA #DesarrolloAI #InteligenciaArtificial)

## What Works

- Structured lessons with a clear take-away
- Before/after stories with a concrete resolution
- Content that explains something non-obvious in plain language

## What Fails

- Pure opinion without specifics
- Lists without explanation ("5 things I learned" with no depth)
- Exclamation-heavy motivational content
