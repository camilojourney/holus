SYNTHESIS: KEY RECOMMENDATIONS FOR B2B AI TOOL COMPANIES
================================================================================

FREQUENCY:
- Company page: 2-3 posts/week (8-12/month). Do NOT exceed 1 post per 24 hours.
- Founder/team personal profiles: 3-5 posts/week each for employee advocacy.
- Consistency over months matters more than raw frequency.
- Content has a 48-72 hour lifespan; spacing posts preserves reach.

CONTENT MIX (recommended weekly for company page at 3 posts/week):
- 1x PDF carousel / document post (technical tutorial, case study, product walkthrough) -- highest engagement at 6.6-7.0%
- 1x Thought leadership post (industry insight, founder perspective, bold take) -- 2-3x engagement vs. standard brand posts
- 1x Product/company update (launch, milestone, team highlight) -- keeps page active

RATIO: 70-80% educational/insightful, 10-20% curated/community, 10-15% promotional

FORMAT RANKINGS (by engagement rate, 2026):
1. Native documents / PDF carousels: 6.60-7.00% engagement
2. Multi-image posts: Strong likes/engagement
3. Text + image posts: Moderate
4. Text-only posts: <2% engagement
5. Video: Declining reach (deprioritized by algorithm in 2025-2026)
6. Polls: Best for pages with 50k+ followers

CRITICAL INSIGHT FOR AI COMPANIES:
- Employee advocacy (personal profiles) is now the primary distribution channel because company page organic reach dropped 60-66% since 2024.
- AI-generated content is penalized (-47% reach); use AI for drafts but humanize before publishing.
- LinkedIn is #2 most-cited source in AI search engines -- original, knowledge-driven articles (500-2000 words) get cited most.
- 79% of hidden B2B buyers prefer vendors with strong thought leadership.
