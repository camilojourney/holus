# UX Spec: Holus Content Studio

## Purpose

Make `/content` feel like a content creation application, not an observability page. The primary job is: one thought becomes a reviewable, schedulable campaign package across LinkedIn, Instagram, Reels, Threads, X, and Facebook.

## Primary User

A solo founder/operator who wants to create and approve social content quickly without thinking about internal agent plumbing first.

## Experience Principles

- Creation first: the thought composer is the primary action.
- Output visibility: the latest campaign package must show platform drafts, image/reel assets, quality gates, and text previews.
- Human gate: approval and scheduling prepare content for the queue; publishing still requires the approved boundary.
- Agent confidence, not agent clutter: show involved agents inline with the campaign, and keep the full 38-agent fleet available as an expandable audit layer.
- Work surface over monitoring: queue counts and review cards support the creation flow instead of becoming the whole page.

## Layout

1. Header: explain the job in customer language.
2. Creation cockpit: thought intake plus selected formats.
3. Workflow rail: Thought -> Formats -> Assets -> Review -> Schedule.
4. Latest campaign package: previews of copy, images, reel scripts, platforms, agents, and quality checks.
5. Review and schedule queue: cards for outstanding work.
6. Expandable Holus operating system: full registry grouped by function.

## Safety Copy

Use local, accurate labels:

- "Approve draft" instead of "Approve & Post Now"
- "Schedule draft" instead of implying direct social publishing
- Explain that publishing remains gated and does not bypass human review
