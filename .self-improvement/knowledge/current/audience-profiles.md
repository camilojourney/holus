## Primary Audience: Consulting Prospects

### Who They Are

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Titles | CTO, VP Engineering, Head of AI/ML, Technical Founder, Director of Engineering |
| Company size | 50-500 employees |
| Industries | Tech-adjacent companies considering AI transformation — SaaS, fintech, health tech, media, e-commerce |
| Geography | NYC metro (initial), expanding to remote/national |
| Budget authority | Can approve $10K-100K consulting engagements |
| AI maturity | Early to mid — have experimented with ChatGPT/Copilot, haven't shipped production AI yet |

### What They're Dealing With

| Pain Point | Internal Symptom | What They Search For |
|------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Board pressure for AI strategy | "We need an AI roadmap by Q3" | "AI strategy for mid-size companies" |
| POC-to-production gap | Built a demo, can't get it into production | "How to deploy AI in production" |
| Hiring is too slow/expensive | Posted ML engineer roles, no qualified candidates | "AI consultant vs hiring AI team" |
| Vendor overwhelm | 50 AI vendors pitched this quarter, can't evaluate | "How to evaluate AI vendors" |
| Failed first attempt | Spent $200K on an AI project that didn't ship | "Why AI projects fail" |
| Competitor anxiety | Rival launched an AI feature, board is asking questions | "AI implementation timeline" |

### What Triggers Them to Act

| Trigger | Signal | Timeline |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Budget approval for AI initiatives | New fiscal year, special allocation | Immediate — looking for help now |
| New CTO/VP who wants to modernize | Leadership change, mandate to innovate | 1-3 months — building relationships |
| Competitor shipped an AI feature | Board email: "why don't we have this?" | Urgent — want to respond fast |
| Failed AI POC | Post-mortem, team demoralized | Ready — need someone to fix it |
| Industry conference exposure | Came back from a conference inspired/overwhelmed | 1-2 months — digesting, researching |
| Regulation requiring AI compliance | New rules around AI use in their industry | 3-6 months — longer cycle |

### Where They Are

| Channel | How They Use It | Implication for Holus |
|---------|----------------|----------------------|
| LinkedIn feed | Scroll during commute, between meetings. Read 2-3 long posts daily. | Hook must stop the scroll in <2 seconds |
| LinkedIn search | Search "AI implementation", "AI consulting" when evaluating | SEO-aware content: use terms they'd search |
| LinkedIn DMs | Reach out to people whose content resonated | Soft CTA: "DM me" works better than links |
| Industry Slack/Discord | Ask peers for consultant recommendations | Not directly reachable — build word-of-mouth |
| Conferences/meetups (NYC) | Network, attend talks, collect business cards | In-person reinforces online authority |
| Google search | "AI consulting firms NYC", "how to hire AI consultant" | Secondary — LinkedIn is the discovery channel |

### What Content Resonates

| Content Type | Why It Works | Example |
|-------------|-------------|---------|
| Builder stories with real architecture | Proves expertise, not just theory | "Here's the actual schema behind my AI experiment memory" |
| Frameworks with numbered steps | Actionable, save-worthy, shareable | "The 3-question test for AI readiness" |
| Contrarian takes backed by experience | Shows independent thinking, sparks discussion | "Stop hiring AI engineers. Train the ones you have." |
| Before/after with real metrics | Undeniable proof of capability | "247 tests, 3 production systems, 0 downtime" |
| Industry commentary with insider lens | Shows landscape awareness | "Everyone's building agents. Nobody's building the infrastructure." |

### What Turns Them Off

| Anti-Pattern | Why It Fails |
|-------------|-------------|
| Generic AI hype ("AI will transform everything") | They've heard it 1000 times. No signal. |
| Feature announcements without business context | They don't care about your product. They care about their problem. |
| Guru energy ("I'll 10x your business") | CTOs smell BS instantly. Credibility = 0. |
| Walls of buzzwords | "Leverage AI-driven synergies" — they stop reading |
| No proof / all claims | "I'm the best AI consultant" without showing the work |
| Overly salesy CTAs | "Book a call NOW" in every post — feels desperate |

### How to Convert Them

```
Awareness (scroll LinkedIn, see content)
  → "This person knows what they're talking about"
  → Follow, read more posts over 2-4 weeks

Consideration (engage with content)
  → Comment on a post, save a framework
  → Visit profile, read headline + about section
  → "I wonder if they could help us"

Intent (reach out)
  → DM: "We're dealing with [exact problem you described]. Can we talk?"
  → Or: click discovery call link in profile

Conversion (discovery call)
  → Camilo demonstrates expertise in the call
  → Proposes engagement (assessment, implementation, review)
```

**Content-to-conversion timeline:** 2-6 weeks of consistent posting before DMs start.
Patience is key. Authority compounds — each post adds to the cumulative impression.

## Cross-Audience Strategy

### The Bridge: Products → Consulting

Every product audience interaction is a potential consulting proof point:

```
Product content: "I built genpeli to edit videos in one command"
  → Consulting reframe: "Here's how I automated a 4-hour workflow with AI"
  → Prospect reaction: "Could he automate our workflows too?"
```

The marketing agent should always think about the dual audience:
- **Product users** see: "Cool tool, I want to try it"
- **Consulting prospects** see: "This person builds real AI systems"

### Content Allocation

| Audience | % of Content | Why |
|----------|-------------|-----|
| Consulting prospects (LinkedIn-first) | 70% | Primary pipeline. Every post builds authority. |
| Product users (repurposed) | 20% | Brand building. Products-as-proof stories. |
| Mixed (product story → consulting angle) | 10% | Bridge content that serves both audiences. |

## Engagement Patterns (What the Agent Should Track)

### High-Signal Engagement (Consulting Prospects)

| Signal | What It Means | Agent Action |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| Comment with company context | "We're dealing with this at [company]" | Flag for Camilo — potential lead |
| DM after reading post | Direct interest in consulting | Flag immediately — warm lead |
| Save + share combination | Content was reference-worthy | Note the format/topic — create more like it |
| Profile view spike after post | Hook worked, credibility check in progress | Ensure profile is optimized |
| Connection request with note | "Loved your post about X" | Track — nurture over next posts |

### Low-Signal Engagement (Ignore for Strategy)

| Signal | Why It's Noise |
|--------|---------------|
| Likes without comments | Low-effort, doesn't indicate prospect interest |
| Follows from unrelated industries | Vanity metric |
| Generic comments ("Great post!") | Bots or courtesy — no pipeline signal |
| High impressions, low engagement | Content reached people but didn't resonate |
