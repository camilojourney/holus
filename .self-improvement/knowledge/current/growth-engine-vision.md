## What Holus MUST Become

Holus is not just a "content poster." It is a **social media growth engine** that:

1. **Analyzes what's working in the niche** — scrapes/reads top-performing posts in AI tools, ComfyUI, developer tools spaces
2. **Extracts frameworks from top performers** — reverse-engineers why certain posts go viral (hooks, structure, CTAs)
3. **Creates content based on proven viral patterns** — uses frameworks, not random generation
4. **Generates scroll-stopping hooks automatically** — the first line/3 seconds decide everything
5. **Matches the founder's exact voice and tone** — sounds like Camilo, not ChatGPT
6. **Analyzes competitors' best-performing posts** — studies what works for similar tools

## The Loop That Beats the Algorithm

```
OLD: Post → Hope → Wait → Nothing
NEW: Analyze → Create → Publish → Track → Learn → Scale
```

## Target Results (Benchmarks to Chase)

- 1M+ organic views per week (across all platforms)
- 30+ inbound leads per month
- Content creation: 10 minutes per piece (not 3 hours)
- Posting frequency: 5x/week minimum per platform
- Voice consistency: content sounds like the founder, not generic AI

## What This Means for the Agent

### The agent MUST:
- Study the niche BEFORE creating content (not just generate blind)
- Analyze competitors in the AI tools space (CivitAI, Runway, Descript, etc.)
- Extract viral frameworks: hook types, post structures, CTA patterns
- Learn the founder's voice from existing content and social media history
- Track EVERY post's performance in a database (not just logs)
- Run weekly analysis: what worked, what didn't, why
- Double down on what works, kill what doesn't

### Content Framework Library (to build over time)
- **The Breakdown** → "Here's how X actually works" (tutorial)
- **The Contrarian** → "Everyone does X. Here's why Y is better" (hot take)
- **The Before/After** → "My workflow before vs after [product]" (proof)
- **The Thread** → "5 things I learned from X" (value list)
- **The Behind-the-Scenes** → "How I built X in Y days" (founder journey)
- **The Engagement Bait** → "Comment 'AI' and I'll send it" (CTA)
- **The Data Drop** → "I analyzed 1000 posts. Here's what works" (authority)

### Hook Templates (proven patterns)
- "R.I.P [old way]. [New thing] just broke it."
- "Stop [common mistake]. Do this instead."
- "I spent [time] analyzing [thing]. Here's what nobody tells you."
- "Most [audience] are still [old approach]. That's why they [pain]."
- "[Tool] changed the game when you use it properly."
- "The [audience] who [outcome] all do this one thing."
